Care & Technical Details

How our tiles are made, how the bronze ages, and everything you need to plan, install, and care for a Foundry Art project — in one place.

The proof behind the piece

How our tiles are made

Every Foundry Art pattern began as a single hand-carved master, refined through dozens of revisions. Every tile since is cast from that original — a true copy of the first carving, not a copy of a copy. What follows is the work of many hands.

A living finish

Bronze becomes more beautiful with age.

Solid bronze is not sealed behind a coating, so it keeps changing after it leaves us. Traditional Bronze grows warm, mellow, and darker than the day it arrived; White Bronze stays close to its original cooler tone. Neither is a flaw to prevent — it is the material doing what bronze has done for five thousand years.

To slow the natural patina, a light coat of paste wax is all it takes. To restore the original brightness, the surface polishes back easily and quickly with a soft Scotch-Brite pad.

Day-to-day care

For simple cleaning, wipe down with a soft cloth and mild dish soap. Avoid abrasive pads and harsh chemicals for routine cleaning; the patina is part of the bronze, not dirt on top of it.

Traditional Bronze vs. White Bronze

Traditional Bronze is an alloy of copper and tin, the same alloy typically used for casting fine sculpture. Its natural patina develops gradually, growing warm, mellow, and darker than when the tiles first arrive. Our White Bronze alloy has less copper and more zinc and nickel, giving it a cooler color similar to nickel silver, and it stays close to its original tone over time.
