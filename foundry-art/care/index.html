What will the patina look like?

01

Bronze becomes more beautiful with age.

We believe the natural patina is the key to the deep beauty of bronze. Technically it is oxidation — bronze responding to the oxygen in the atmosphere. In practice, it is the surface darkening and mellowing the way a prized antique does, with time, weather and wear each playing a part.

It is not a flaw to be prevented. It is the material doing what bronze has always done.

Like the shiny nose of a well-loved statue, this is a beautiful reward that comes only with age.

Ted Lowitz, founder

02

Footsteps do the polishing.

When used in a floor, time-worn paths appear as the tiles are gradually polished by footsteps. The high points brighten where people walk while the recesses keep deepening — the pattern gets more legible, not less.

It is the one kind of wear you cannot buy and cannot fake.

03

Water speeds everything up.

Constant water exposure drives a very dark patina, and outdoors it will eventually turn the opaque green you know from weathered bronze. If you want to hold the finish closer to new, seal it with lacquer and plan on reapplying periodically.

Neither outcome harms the bronze. Wet or dry, the tile underneath stays solid for generations.

04

Let it age, slow it down, or keep it bright.

How far the patina goes is entirely up to you. Bronze asks very little — a soft cloth for everyday life, and a few minutes of attention if you ever want to steer it.
