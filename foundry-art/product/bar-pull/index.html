Day-to-day care

Wipe down with a soft cloth and mild dish soap. Avoid abrasive pads, acid-based cleaners, and bleach — all will damage the finish over time. The patina you see is part of the bronze; it deepens gracefully with age.

If it gets dull

A drop of mineral oil on a microfiber cloth, buffed in small circles, restores the warmth of the Traditional finish. The White finish does not require oiling.
