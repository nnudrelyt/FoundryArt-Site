Day-to-day care

For simple cleaning, wipe down with a soft cloth and mild dish soap.

As it ages

Bronze becomes more beautiful with age, growing more mellow and darkening over time. A light coat of paste wax will slow down the natural patina process.

To restore the original brightness

Tiles may be polished easily and quickly with a soft Scotch-Brite pad.
