Frequently asked questions

Most Foundry Art orders ship within 5–7 business days from our Chicago studio. Very large orders may take longer to produce. Every order ships insured, direct from our workshop to you.

Yes. Registered customers can request a complimentary color-swatch set — 2″ cut pieces of White Bronze and Traditional Bronze — shipped USPS. Registered customers may also request up to two full tiles at full price, with free shipping in both directions.

Traditional Bronze is an alloy of copper and tin, the same alloy typically used for casting fine sculpture. Its natural patina develops gradually, growing warm, mellow, and darker than when the tiles first arrive. Our White Bronze alloy has less copper and more zinc and nickel, giving it a cooler color similar to nickel silver, and it stays close to its original tone over time.

Yes. Registered trade professionals receive discounted pricing on all tile and hardware. Contact the studio to apply for a trade account.

For simple cleaning, wipe down with a soft cloth and mild dish soap. Bronze becomes more beautiful with age, growing more mellow and darkening as it ages. To restore the original brightness, tiles may be polished easily and quickly with a soft Scotch-Brite pad. A light coat of paste wax will slow down the natural patina process.
