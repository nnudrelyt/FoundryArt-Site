Frequently asked questions

Most Foundry Art orders ship within 5–7 business days from our Chicago studio. Custom or hand-finished pieces may take 2–3 weeks. Every order ships insured, direct from our workshop to you.

Traditional Bronze is our warm, tobacco-toned finish that develops a deeper patina over time. White Bronze is a cooler, silvery-pewter finish that stays close to its original tone. Both are cast from the same solid bronze alloy.
