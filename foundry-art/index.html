The hands behind every tile

The work of many hands.
Made to outlast the room.

Each Foundry Art tile represents the work of many hands — from the artisan who carves the master, to the team who pours the bronze, to the people who finish, pack, and ship it from our Chicago studio.

1
Carved by studio artisansEvery design begins as a hand-carved master in our Chicago studio — refined through dozens of revisions before it’s ever cast.
2
Cast in solid bronzeMolten bronze poured by hand from the master. Solid through — not a coating, not a plating — the same alloy people have valued for 5,000 years.
3
Cared for end-to-endHand-finished, personally inspected, and supported by the people who made it. Questions before install or years later? The studio is one call away.
