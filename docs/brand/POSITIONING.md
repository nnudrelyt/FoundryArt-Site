## Brand pillars (Roadmap framing)

- **Promise** — The only American studio hand-casting solid bronze accent tile and hardware, sold directly to the consumer.
- **Approach** — Own the uncontested metal category through brand clarity, craft storytelling, and a direct digital presence.
- **Freshness** — The only premium bronze tile brand accessible without a showroom. Metal in search is a white space — it's ours.
- **Experience** — From first discovery to installation, every touchpoint (site, content, guidance) reflects the quality of the product.

## Goal

Double Foundry Art year-over-year ecommerce sales. (Roadmap, page 91.)

## Strategy

**Position the brand for discovery.** Three pillars below.

### 1. Elevate the brand & site experience
- Redesign site to reflect product quality
- Clarify brand hierarchy (Linden / Foundry Art / Claremont)
- Simplify and shorten the path to purchase
- Introduce luxury visual language and typography
- Bring the hero line above the fold immediately

### 2. Own bronze in search & discovery
- Target high-intent bronze + metal tile keywords
- Build a Houzz profile with installed photography
- Establish presence in AI-powered referral (ChatGPT, Perplexity, etc.)
- Launch paid search for category terms
- Develop SEO content around bronze tile use cases

### 3. Tell the craft story
- Studio + maker story — who cast this, and how
- Process photography: carving, casting, finishing
- Lookbook / downloadable buying guide
- Surface "How We Make Our Tiles" content prominently
- Add customer testimonials and case studies

## Categories of competitive differentiation

(See COMPETITIVE.md for the full landscape map.) Foundry Art is unique on **two axes simultaneously**:

1. **Material distinctiveness** — every competitor is ceramic, glass, or stone. Foundry Art is the only one doing solid bronze.
2. **Consumer accessibility** — most competitors are showroom-only or trade-primary. Foundry Art is direct-online with no showroom required.

That quadrant (top-right of the matrix: "Distinct + Accessible") is uncontested.

## The central insight to design against

> *"In a marketplace of established and well-known brands, how does Foundry Art create awareness, earn trust, and purchase from consumers who know the category but don't yet know this brand?"*

Every decision on the site should answer at least one of: awareness, trust, purchase.
