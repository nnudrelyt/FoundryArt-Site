Foundry Art — photo library

Every photo in one place: the web-ready set in assets/images that the build ships, and the full client archive in Foundry Art/_Assets/Photography — the mirror of the Google Drive folders Stacza shares. Archive cards are thumbnails of print-resolution originals; TIF or PSD means it needs converting before it can go on a page. Thumbnails sit on the footer charcoal, so cut-outs show their true edge. lum is mean luminance — the footer ground is 0.177, and the closer a photo sits to it the less correction it needs. Regenerate with python3 docs/photo-library/generate.py.
