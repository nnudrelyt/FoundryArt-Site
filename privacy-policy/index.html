Information we collect

When you place an order, request a sample, or send us a message, we collect the details you give us: your name, email address, shipping and billing address, phone number, and the contents of your order or enquiry.

We also collect basic technical information automatically, such as your browser type, device, and the pages you visit on this site.

How we use it

We use your information to process and ship orders, respond to enquiries, provide customer support, and — where you have asked us to — send occasional studio news.

We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies

This site uses cookies to keep your cart working and to understand how the site is used. You can disable cookies in your browser, though parts of the shop may stop working correctly.

Third parties

We share information only with the services needed to run the business: our payment processor, our shipping carriers, and our email provider. Each handles your data under its own terms.

Your choices

You may ask us to correct or delete the information we hold about you, or to stop sending you email, at any time.

When you choose tile, you make a decision you will live with for a long time. The decision can be daunting. You know what appeals to you today, but what will appeal to you tomorrow? At Linden Workshops, we strive to interpret timeless designs in a personal way. The patterns on our tiles appear in countless variations in art and architecture — from prehistoric stone carvings and classical friezes to modern abstract sculpture. They are timeless designs that resonate in our minds. We hope you will find our tiles to be engagingly new and yet deeply familiar.
